The Story

The air that gives the house its name

Aria Adriatica sits in the Belvedere countryside outside Carovigno, on a gentle hill above the Adriatic. The sea is close enough to walk to in the morning. The house is set back among the century-old olive groves, in the quiet.

The name is a plain description: aria adriatica, the Adriatic air. It comes up from the water through the olive groves — salt at the edges, rosemary underneath — and it is why everyone ends up on the veranda.

They named this countryside Belvedere — the beautiful view — centuries ago. Climb to the rooftop terrace, and you’ll see no one was exaggerating.

The Home

Contemporary with a Mediterranean heartbeat

Space for eight to slow down and stay awhile. A low whitewashed house in the architectural language of southern Puglia, built to disappear into the olive grove. Fully renovated in 2026.

4 bedrooms · 3 bathrooms · sleeps 8. Two primary en-suites at either end of the bedroom wing, two bedrooms sharing a third bath between them, and air-conditioning in every room.

La Pianta · The Plan

A house that climbs with the hill

The house climbs the hill on four levels — parking, pool platform, house, rooftop — with the bedroom wing wrapped around an internal olive courtyard.

The Rooftop

The Bel Vedere

An external staircase climbs to a terrace that covers nearly the entire footprint — about 175 m² of open-air living, a second floor that happens to have no walls. The view runs uninterrupted over the grove to the Adriatic: a thin blue line in the morning, gold at sunset. Long breakfasts, quiet aperitivi, storm-watching in shoulder season.

The Land

Olive groves and the road to the sea

Just over 8,300 m² of olive grove — mature trees, some centuries old — laced with gravel paths, bordered by dry-stone walls, planted with bougainvillea, rosemary and lavender. The beach is a short drive; the protected coves of Torre Guaceto a few minutes north.

Two cats came with the place: Octavia, in her tuxedo, and Augustus, the true Emperor of the villa. They were here before us and remain, as far as they are concerned, the rightful owners. They accept guests.

Mare Nostrum · Our Puglia

A perfect day from the house

Mare nostrum — our sea. Not a tourist list: the bakery we stop at on the way home, the trattoria where we don’t need a menu, the cove where the water is clearest at six in the evening.

  1. 01

    Morning — Pasticceria Prodi dal 1985

    Carovigno · pasticceria

    Amazing pasticciotti, panettone, pandoro. In the summer, head to Torre Santa Sabina for breakfast by the sea at their pop-up, Le Croissant.

  2. 02

    Midday — La Sfornata · Panificio

    Carovigno · bakery

    Warm focaccia and pastries that never make it home. Go early.

  3. 03

    Torre Guaceto

    Protected marine reserve · a short drive

    The beach we send everyone to. Take the little tram down through the macchia to white sand and water so clear you can count your toes. Bring a mask and your bounty from La Sfornata.

  4. 04

    Lunch — Casa Mia

    Carovigno · a few minutes from the city center

    Our table in Carovigno. Honest Pugliese cooking — book outside.

  5. 05

    Afternoon — Apulian Beach Club

    On the coast

    The other kind of beach day: a lounger, a proper spritz, lunch under your umbrella. Reserve ahead in August.

  6. 06

    Aperitivo — The Bel Vedere

    Our rooftop

    If you can pull yourself away from the sea, our favourite terrace is, of course, right upstairs. Ask us for the house spritz recipe — crafted to match the red-orange glow of the Adriatic sun as it dips for the day.

  7.
